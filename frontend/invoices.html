Invoices

Wardens can view invoices. Billing and payments are done by the ADMIN.

Generate invoices

Calls PL/SQL pr_generate_invoice (IN/OUT parameters): rent from the room type + mess charges from fn_meal_charges.

Record payment

The AFTER trigger trg_payment_audit writes the audit row, updates the invoice status and rejects over-payments (ORA-20024).
