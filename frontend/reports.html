Analytical reports

The four window-function reports from database/10_analytics.sql, run live on Oracle.

(i) Running total of mess revenue

SUM(revenue) OVER (PARTITION BY TRUNC(meal_date,'MM') ORDER BY meal_date ROWS BETWEEN UNBOUNDED PRECEDING AND CURRENT ROW)
DayMealsRevenueRunning total

(ii) 7-day moving average of meals served (last 30 days)

AVG(meals_served) OVER (ORDER BY meal_date ROWS BETWEEN 6 PRECEDING AND CURRENT ROW)

(iii) Room occupancy ranking per block

RANK() / DENSE_RANK() OVER (PARTITION BY block_name ORDER BY occupancy_pct DESC)
BlockRoomTypeBedsOccupied%RANKDENSE

(iv) Mess bill trend (LAG / LEAD)

LAG(SUM(mess_amount)) OVER (ORDER BY billing_month)
